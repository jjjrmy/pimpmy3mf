## Bambu Studio printer templates (`web/src/core/bambu/templates/*.json`)

These files are generated by `web/scripts/capture-bambu-templates.py`, which exports projects through
the [Bambu Studio](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio) command-line tool (versions 1.10 and 2.8)
using the printer, process and filament profiles bundled with it. Their settings and G-code come from
those profiles. Bambu Studio is distributed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. The
licensing of these derived template files is under review; they are listed here so it's clear they
originate from Bambu Studio and not from this project.
